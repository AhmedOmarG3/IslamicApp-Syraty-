# Religious Content Integrity Contract

- Quran and Azkar assets are approved inputs, never AI-generated material.
- Record source attribution, reviewer, structure rules, and fingerprints.
- Validate presence, parsing, ordering, required fields, verse relationships, and exact/canonical text identity.
- Intentional differences require source evidence, tests, and Ahmed's approval.
- UI work must not rewrite, normalize, translate, paraphrase, or silently correct source text.
